How it works arrow_forward Contact
Do I have to do a long task for every job?

No. Many jobs accept your existing verified skill scores, so you can apply in one click. Standard assessments are short and reusable across jobs, and every task shows its estimated effort before you start (screening tasks ≤ 2h, company assessments ≤ 8h).

How is my score calculated?

A reviewer scores your work against a published 100-point rubric. Your track score is 10 × the difficulty-weighted average of your best score per task, calculated separately for each skill track. Full methodology.

Can I use AI tools?

Each task states its policy: allowed, allowed with disclosure, or not allowed. Using AI where it's allowed is never treated as cheating.

What if I think my score is wrong?

Dispute it within 30 days. Unresume moderators — not the company — review it and can uphold, adjust or void the score.

Is it free for candidates?

Yes. Applying, taking assessments and keeping your verified scores and certificates are always free. Companies will pay for scale; see proposed plans.

Who owns my work?

You do. Companies may use a submission only to evaluate you for that role, and you choose who sees each portfolio item.
