Template terms reflecting how the platform works. Have them reviewed by qualified counsel before launch.

1. Accounts

You must give accurate information and keep your password secure. Company accounts must be created by people authorised to hire for that company; academy accounts by people authorised to act for that institution. Companies and academies are verified by Unresume before they can publish jobs or refer students.

2. Tasks and submissions

  • Each job is applied to by completing its published task. The time limit starts when you press Start assessment and keeps running if you close the page.
  • Job tasks can be attempted once; practice tasks may be retried if time runs out.
  • You keep ownership of everything you submit. You grant the company that owns the task a licence to review it for hiring purposes only. Companies must not use task submissions as unpaid production work.
  • You control who sees your final output: Public, Recruiters only, or Only me. Outputs of tasks a company marks confidential can't be made public.
  • Effort limits: screening tasks are capped at 2 hours of estimated work, company assessments at 8 hours, and every task shows its estimate before you start. Jobs may accept your existing verified scores instead of a new task.
  • Permitted use: a company may use your submission only to evaluate you for that role. It may not ship, publish or resell it. Companies confirm this when they publish a task; report any task that looks like real production work.

3. Assessment integrity

AI tools: each task states its policy — allowed, allowed with disclosure, or not allowed. Using AI where it is allowed is never treated as cheating; disclose it where asked.

Submit only your own work. You may use public documentation and libraries, and starter material the task provides. Not allowed: submitting someone else's work, sharing your work with other candidates of the same task, having another person complete the task, or using tools a task explicitly forbids. Unresume fingerprints files, compares submissions and records timing and tab-switch signals to detect misconduct. Automated flags and reviewer concerns are always reviewed by a person — no one is suspended or banned on an automated result alone. Confirmed misconduct can lead to a voided score, closed applications and account suspension. You can appeal a void within 30 days; a different moderator reviews appeals and may ask for a short live walkthrough.

4. Scores, ranks and disputes

  • A verified score is awarded by a company reviewer against the task's published 100-point rubric. Your Skill Score is 10 × the average of your verified scores. Rankings require a minimum number of verified tasks.
  • Portfolio likes, followers and external projects never affect scores.
  • You may dispute a score within 30 days. Disputes are decided by Unresume moderators, who may uphold, adjust or void the score. Their decision is final on the platform.

5. Companies

Companies must publish accurate job and task information, keep tasks within the effort limits, score every submission fairly against the stated rubric and within the review target (currently 5 days), note authenticity concerns rather than penalising on suspicion, not discriminate unlawfully, and use candidate data only for hiring. Rubrics are locked once a task has submissions, so all candidates are judged the same way.

6. Academies

Academies may add only students who are enrolled with them. Linking a student's Unresume account requires the student's consent, students can leave an academy at any time, and academies never control a student's account or verified history. Referrals are limited by the slots each company sets. When a student is placed or leaves, their Student ID converts to an independent Candidate ID and the academy loses priority-referral status for them.

7. Acceptable use

No harassment, spam, fake profiles, scraping, or attempts to bypass security. Report problems with the Report buttons on profiles, listings and chats.

8. Suspension and termination

We may suspend accounts that break these terms. You can request deletion at any time from Settings; retention follows the Privacy Policy.

9. Liability

Unresume provides the platform "as is". Hiring decisions are made by companies, not by Unresume. To the extent allowed by law, our liability is limited to the fees you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.
