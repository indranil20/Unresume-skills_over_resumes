1. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, password (stored as a salted hash), role and organisation.
  • Profile data you add: bio, location, skills, portfolio links.
  • Assessment data: files and links you submit for tasks, notes, start and submit times, and integrity signals described in section 4.
  • Scores and feedback given by company reviewers, and dispute records.
  • Hiring activity: applications, interview schedules, messages with companies and academies.
  • Academy records: if your academy adds you, your name, email, batch and Student ID.
  • Technical data: sign-in times and actions recorded in our audit log for security and dispute handling.

2. How we use it

To run the platform: deliver tasks, calculate your verified Skill Score, show your portfolio, let companies review your work and contact you, let academies track students who enrol with them, detect cheating, resolve disputes, and keep the service secure. We do not sell personal data and we do not use your submissions to train third-party AI models.

3. Who can see what

4. Assessment integrity

While you take a task we record: when you started and submitted, how often you left the assessment tab, and a cryptographic fingerprint (SHA-256) of each file you upload. We compare fingerprints, links and notes with other submissions to detect copying. Automated flags are always reviewed by a person before any action is taken, and you can dispute outcomes.

5. Retention

  • Account and profile data: until you delete your account.
  • Submissions and scores tied to an application: 90 days after account deletion, to resolve open disputes, then deleted.
  • Audit logs: 2 years, for security investigations.
  • Contact-form messages: 1 year.

6. Your rights & controls

In Settings you can download all your data as JSON, change profile and score visibility, control who can message you, choose notifications, and request account deletion. You may also ask us to correct inaccurate data or object to processing by contacting us.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, access is role-based, and every state-changing action is audit-logged. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and regulators of any breach as required by law.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: contact form or privacy@unresume.io. Grievance officer: Unresume, Kolkata, India.
